Our Story

Preto's Island Restaurant & Bar is a family owned business rooted in Cape Verdean culture, passion and tradition. Since 2018, we have brought the rich flavors of our heritage to downtown Brockton.

Come in after work for cocktails, gather the family for dinner, plan a special event or enjoy a date night. Our menus pair quality food at good prices with a thoughtful selection of wine, spirits and cocktails.
